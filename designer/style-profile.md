# Style profile: Heraldo

Living file. Update after every reaction or hand edit. "(inferred)" = not yet confirmed.

## Communication
- Chats in casual Jakarta Indonesian (gw/lu, dong, njir). Reply in the same register, short and direct.
- Prefers reacting to a built sample over moodboards.
- Ask at most 1-2 short questions; otherwise decide and show.

## Loves
- Designs that carry a story: layout and visual choices should say something, not just look nice.
- Deliberate misalignment as a device (e.g. an off-centre "slop" strip). Never auto-fix his oddities.
- Headlines that lead; he controls headline line breaks himself.
- Expressive, word-meaning fonts: fast words get fast fonts (Edu TAS Beginner for "Kurir"), water/rain words get smooth rounded fonts (Madimi One for "Hujan"); Hanuman for section titles, Happy Monkey for route numerals (Kurir Hujan, 2026-11-02). Instrument Serif felt too stiff.
- Centered hero with descriptive text directly beneath; bottom-of-hero gradient blending into the next section (intro page).
- Meaningful motion: continuity over clean cuts (camera carries the title away), text tied to the object it describes, "signal/glitch" reveals over soft blur-rises, lit trails over dimming the past, typing-feel per-word appears, dissolve bookends (Jitter edits, inferred).
- Carousels and scroll motion are fine when interruptible and the scroll carries meaning (JalaWatch).
- Subtle spring/easing motion matters to him; untouched artboards mean "no objection".
- Functional numbers and labels are fine (project/process numbers, step labels).

## Hates / avoid
- Decorative eyebrow labels, kickers, small top-corner labels, decorative section numbers (01/02), rules/lines under headings, redundant captions.
- Generic AI slop: default purple gradients, gradient-blob heroes, emoji bullets, centered-everything, Inter-on-white, glassmorphism for decoration, filler icons.
- Palettes he rejected: Swiss red/blue/paper, warm cream/terracotta. Black+green terminal: overused (bored, not hated).
- Overused palette: ink #12233F / bone #EEF1F3 / ochre #D9A21B / steel #5B6B80. Don't reuse by default.
- Night blue + sodium yellow felt "meh". Generic signage style "lacks character, many sites use that".
- Patching an old codebase for a redesign (messy, wastes tokens).

## Tools and formats
- Web: design in Figma -> animate in Jitter -> code agent builds from scratch from the Figma frames.
- Posters: Canva (so he can edit). Decks: Google Slides on a proper theme/master (not Figma Slides); explain the theme setup before building.
- He may leave Figma elements static because he plans to animate them in Jitter.
- Ibadah lyric decks: his template, soprano lyrics only, Indonesian translation in the lower text box, right-aligned.
